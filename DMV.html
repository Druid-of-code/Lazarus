Teen Wellness Center (TWC) Services (Alexandria) (en español)

The Teen Wellness Center is a medical clinic created to provide accessible health services to anyone between the ages of 12-19 living in the City of Alexandria

Confidential services provided
  • Pregnancy testing and prevention
  • Diagnosis and treatment of sexually transmitted infections (Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, HIV, syphilis, and herpes).
  • Reproductive health services (birth control)
  • Health education on nutrition, exercise, and stress reduction techniques
  • Mental health and substance use screening and referrals
Contact information and Locations

Ryan Bartel Foundation

Programs offered
  • The FORT - a space for young people in middle and high school to find connection and participate activities that help them deal with the stresses of everyday life
  • FORT At School - a free in-school program designed as a shortened FORT workshop where teens can unwind and explore activities that support mental wellness with their peers.
  • FORT Teen Camp - camps filled games, activities, and workshops centered on mental wellness
  • FORT Teen Hangout - a place where teens (14-18) can discuss topics such as school pressures, loneliness, relationships, and anxiety
