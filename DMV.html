Teen Wellness Center (TWC) Services (Alexandria)

A wellness center dedicated to providing help to numerous health concerns that include vaccines, sexual health, and mental health. Their sexual health and mental health help, among other things, remains confidential but things like vaccines are non-confidential and need parental consent.

More information

Fairfax-Falls Church Community Services Board

Helps those struggling with mental health, substance use, and developmental disabilities. Payments scale with their ability to pay scale, which can make it cheaper then private therapy especially if you don't have insurance.

More information

Free2Talk

EveryMind Youth & Family Services

They serve families in Montgomery County and Prince George's County. They offer services that are preventative and more intense when needed.

Contact: 301-424-0656

Visit their website

Montgomery County Crisis Center

Open 24/7, they provide emergency mental health care. No need for an appointment, just show up to 1301 Piccard Drive, South Entrance, Rockville or call 240-777-4000. Walk-in services and calls are free, although you can only get help if you are a Montgomery county resident and you must bring a photo ID to show proof of residency.

More information

Identity-youth.org

Helps underserved communities and their teens adapt to and transition into adulthood. Their services are well rounded and can range from academic and family-case help to non-clinical emotional support and mental help.
You can request their services using their website or by calling 301-800-5519

Visit their website

Latin American Youth Center (Montgomery and Prince George's counties)

They offer mental health counseling for those who are struggling wether it be substance use, family issues, or depression. Some of their staff are also bilingual.
